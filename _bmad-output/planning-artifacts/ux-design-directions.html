Ledger Dark
Dense, information-first layout. Full sidebar with text labels. Stats lead the Overview with a 4-column grid. Warm amber accents mark the active state. Rough.js borders appear on all cards. This is the most "SaaS dashboard" feeling direction — but warm and differentiated from Vercel through palette and typeface.
Recommended starting point Dense Full sidebar Stats-led overview Rough.js borders

Direction 03
Journal Grid
Grid-first layout inspired by newspaper and notebook composition. Stats arranged as 4-column journal cards with a ruled underline accent. Sidebar is minimal with section grouping labels. Emphasizes the editorial, handmade quality over the SaaS dashboard structure. Lower "tech startup" reading.
Editorial Grouped nav Grid cards Newspaper feel

Leans into the developer-portfolio-as-tool aesthetic. Navigation uses monospace with command-prefix style labels. Overview shows structured output-log style data. Very high "technical" signal — but risks losing non-technical visitors (Sarah) quickly. Strong Marcus appeal. Most polarizing direction.

Direction 06
Sparse Overview
Full-bleed grid layout with no cards — page itself is divided into zones by hairline borders. Map gets equal billing with the intro copy on the hero row. Content fills the viewport edge-to-edge. Most magazine-like, least SaaS-like. Very different from all other directions. Risk: could feel like a landing page rather than a dashboard product.

// elements worth mixing
01 sidebar + 02 icon mode
Full sidebar that collapses to icon-only on smaller viewports (already planned) — Dir 01 expanded, Dir 02 collapsed.
01 stats + 06 map hero
Stats row at top (Dir 01), but map gets its own prominent zone below — reinforcing work-first intent.
03 grouped nav + 01 density
Dir 03's "Explore / Connect" nav grouping inside Dir 01's full sidebar. Adds structure without adding visual weight.
05 avatar (admin only)
Dir 05's avatar+name treatment fits the admin panel better than the public portfolio — shows the owner identity in a context where it makes sense.
